Privacy, in plain language.

This policy explains what information AI Marketing Labs collects through this website, why we use it and the choices available to you.

When you request a training release, ask for a live-session update or contact us, you may provide your name, work email, WhatsApp number and information about your business. The registration record may also include its page source, consent version and permitted campaign parameters such as UTM values or advertising click identifiers. Information collected automatically

If you allow analytics, those services may collect basic device, browser, page-view and interaction data and may use cookies or similar technology under their own terms. Browser storage is also used to remember your analytics choice, permitted attribution details and funnel version.

How we use information

  • To protect the site and prevent misuse.

Service providers and disclosure

We may use service providers for forms, hosting, analytics and communications. For example, current forms may send contact fields to Google Forms. We share information only as needed to operate those services, comply with law, protect rights or complete a business transfer. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above or to meet legal obligations. No internet service can promise absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to limit access and protect the information we hold.

Your choices

You may ask what information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or ask us to stop direct marketing. Some requests may be limited by legal or record-keeping obligations. You can also clear browser storage or block cookies in your browser.

International processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their privacy protections may differ from those where you live.

Updates and contact

We may update this policy as the website or services change. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions or privacy requests can be sent to the ZippyScale operations inbox at hello@zippyscale.in.
