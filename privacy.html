Privacy policy.

What we collect when you visit, sign up, pay or book. Why we use it. And the rights you have.

AI Marketing Lab is a brand of ZippyScale, a sole proprietorship of Sandeep Kadyan, based in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. ZippyScale is the data fiduciary for your personal data under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Cookies and local storage

The Meta Pixel sets the _fbp and _fbc cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps the ad that brought you (utm tags and fbclid). That's attached to your booking. You can clear these any time in your browser settings, block third-party cookies, or manage ads at facebook.com/adpreferences.

Who processes it for us

  • Razorpay (payments, India)
  • Cal.com (booking, US and EU)
  • Meta Platforms (Pixel and Conversions API, US)
  • Google (Apps Script, which relays sign-up, payment and booking events to Meta; Google Sheets, which stores sign-ups; email, US)
  • GitHub Pages (website hosting) and jsDelivr (video player script)

Some of these store data outside India. We share only what each one needs. We don't sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Payment and invoice records: as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Contact and booking details: while you're a lead or a client, then deleted once no longer needed, or sooner if you ask.
  • Ad and visit data: under Meta's own retention rules; local storage stays until you clear it.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to do these things.

  • tell you what personal data we hold and who we've shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it (unless the law makes us keep it, such as payment records);
  • stop using it for marketing, by withdrawing consent;
  • name someone to act for you if you die or become unable to.
