# AI Marketing Lab

register: brand

Written from `Sandy/company.yaml`, `Sandy/memory/aiml-brand-remy-system.md` and the live offer docs
on 16 Sep 2026, not from an interview. Sandy is the claim authority: nothing here is invented.

## Product purpose

A free 90 minute live session, run Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday at 6:00pm IST and Sunday at
11:00am IST, first session Sunday 20 September 2026. On it Sandy builds the thing that brings
clients in, live on screen. At the end he explains the paid six week Sprint, $200, sold as a group
cohort. Nothing is charged on the page: the page sells the seat, the session sells the Sprint.

## Users

Three distinct buyers, who must each find their own sentence. A blended callout is addressed to
nobody.

- **Marketing agency owner.** Their nouns: retainer, pipeline, referrals, scope, client churn.
  The pain: the pipeline goes quiet exactly when they are busiest delivering.
- **High ticket coach.** Their nouns: booked calls, no shows, cohort, enrolment, DMs.
  The pain: needs booked calls this month, not more followers.
- **Service business owner.** Their nouns: footfall, enquiries, rebooking, season, capacity.
  The pain: one season carries the year, enquiries go cold overnight.

Their vocabulary comes from `offers/sprint/research/india-voices-2026-08/RAW.md`, 2,114 lines of
quotes posted publicly by real owners. Never write a pain line for an ICP without a quote from that
ICP open in front of you.

## Brand

AI Marketing Lab is Sandy Kadyan personally, not an agency. ZippyScale is the agency and the two
never mix in copy. Positioning: AI automated marketing operations running on Claude Code. Never
"connecting ChatGPT and n8n", which is both false and small.

The visual system is AI with Remy's, adopted whole on 28 Aug 2026 and ratified 12 Sep, with one
substitution: his blue becomes Petronas teal. Locked. See DESIGN.md.

## Tone

Plain, specific, and slightly understated. The reader is a working owner in India who has been sold
to constantly and believes almost none of it. Market sophistication is stage 4 to 5: their own words
are "everyone is promising 100x better results", so a claim-led opener cannot work. Identification
and a visible mechanism, or nothing.

Rules that hold everywhere: no em dashes. No exclamation marks. No adjectives where a number would
do. Say the price. Say what it costs in time. Name the thing that goes wrong.

## Anti-references

- Guru launch pages: countdown timers that reset, fake seat counters, "only 3 spots left".
- Any scarcity that is not literally true. A cap that exists because of real delivery capacity is
  honest; a timer that resets on refresh is not, and it does not go on the page.
- Stock photography of people in headsets, laptop-on-a-beach imagery, hustle language.
- Icon-title-subtitle card grids repeated down a page.
- Testimonials or client wins that Sandy has not personally confirmed. Three previously canonical
  client results were wrong and were removed; do not reintroduce them.
- Saheb (arisen.com) and Oslo Furniture never appear on any page. Context only.

## Strategic principles

1. **Proof must be checkable by a stranger.** Payment notifications from the inbox that received
   them, cropped so the amount is readable. Not adjectives.
2. **One action per page.** The hero's action and the closing action are the same action.
3. **The page never takes money.** No payment link. Sandy closes live on the session.
4. **Send the wrong people away in plain words.** The filter section must read as candid, which is
   why it carries the least motion on the page.
5. **Every section states the job it does for the buyer**, in a `data-job` attribute, and fails its
   gate if it cannot.
