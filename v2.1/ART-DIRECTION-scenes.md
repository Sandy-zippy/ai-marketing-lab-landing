# Art direction: the two v2.1 scenes (problem, why)

Same art world as the approved v1 scenes: the petrol stage of s4 (radial petrol gradient, 32px grid floor masked to the centre), teal-hi #3FE0D6 as the only light, Space Mono labels, real depth (Three.js r160 from the same cdnjs URL and SRI the globe uses). One brick-hi element per scene, only at the moment of failure.

## Problem: "The swing" (#problem)
In 5 seconds the viewer must understand: while you chase, this month fills; the moment you stop to deliver, the pipeline drains, so next month arrives empty. Cause, then a one-month-late effect. Time is the x axis. Each month is a 3D slab of day tiles laid out like a contribution graph (weeks run left to right, weekdays front to back). Behind the slabs a translucent pipeline ribbon is extruded in real time by a "now" cursor: it rises while you chase, booked days rise out of the slab as glowing teal columns and the count ticks up. When clients sign, the columns cool to the pale delivery colour, the ribbon falls to the floor, and the camera trucks right into next month: flat tiles, a ribbon on the floor, "0 booked" in brick. The ribbon starts climbing again at the far edge ("back to chasing") as the camera pulls back to show the whole sawtooth. Reference: Stripe and Linear product films (a single continuous camera truck across a data surface) and GitHub's 3D contribution skyline, inside the s4 sprint-block world.

## Why: "The chain" (#why)
In 5 seconds the viewer must understand: a client only arrives if every link holds, and each fix they tried only closed one or two links. A literal 3D chain of six forged links (Attention, Page, Reply, Day 3 and day 7, Call, Client), alternating planes like a real chain, lit teal where the link is closed and dull with a visible gap where it is not. Each pass: the links that fix repairs forge shut (the gap closes with a flash), a glowing lead runs along the chain and falls through the first open link, in brick, with the reason under it; the matching card below lights. The fifth pass, Upstream Downstream, forges the remaining gaps left to right, the lead reaches Client, that link glows and the counter lands on 1. The camera dollies with the lead. On phones the chain hangs vertically so every label stays readable. Reference: Apple product-film close-ups of machined parts (a slow dolly past lit metal), and the physical-chain metaphor in the VSL's script (not its renders).
