# PRODUCT.md: AIML /call

Sources: `Sandy/company.yaml`, `offers/accelerator/funnel/PRODUCT-BIBLE-2026-10-01.md`, memory `aiml-offer-rulings-1oct`, `vsl-funnel-page-spec-1oct`, `lp-feedback-1oct-evening`, `aiml-brand-remy-system`. Every answer below is on disk; nothing here was inferred from a prompt.

## Register
brand (a cold-traffic VSL funnel page: design IS the pitch)

## Users
Marketing agency owners, high-ticket coaches and B2B service founders who already have paying clients (India first, Meta ads traffic, mostly on a phone). They're busy delivering, so leads stop the week they stop pushing.

## Purpose
They watch the VSL and book a ₹999, 45-minute mapping call with Sandy. Everything below the video answers one doubt each: is the call a pitch, what is the system, what the Sprint and the Accelerator are, does it make money, what if it fails, and is it for me.

## Personality
Operator, not guru. Proof over promise. Plain-spoken. A person who has run this for clients since 2017 shows the machine working instead of describing it.

## Anti-references
- Grey rounded card + small illustration + pause button, repeated nine times (the 2 Oct build).
- SaaS hero-metric templates, gradient text, glassmorphism, emoji icons.
- Recycled calendar grid, draining pipeline or dial on every section.
- Entrance fades sold as motion graphics. Motion that hides copy.
- Dark neon AIML v1. Payment screenshots or totals. Program prices.

## Strategic principles
1. The visual carries the section; copy only labels it (about 650 words after the video).
2. Every section reads completely with no motion at all. Motion demonstrates, then settles within about 4 seconds.
3. Phone first: 390px is the primary canvas.
4. One teal accent per surface. Brick red once on the page, for "10 seats".

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast, labels of 12px or more, prefers-reduced-motion shows the finished frame, and any motion over 5 seconds can be paused.
