# Sprint page against aiwithremy.com

Sandy asked twice for this comparison and it was never produced. This is the sprint registration
page (`sprint/index.html`) measured against **aiwithremy.com**, the reference he locked the AIML
design system to on 28 Aug 2026 and ratified on 12 Sep 2026.

Both pages were measured in a real browser on 17 Sep 2026, not described from memory.

## The finding that matters

**Remy's motion is ambient, not illustrative.** His seven keyframes are `cursorBlink`, `cloudDrift`,
`planeFly`, `heroLogosLoop`, `pulse`, `wolScroll` and `heroFadeIn`: a blinking cursor, drifting
clouds, a plane crossing the hero, a looping logo strip, a pulse, an auto-scrolling wall of praise,
and one fade. Nothing on his page draws itself. He carries the page on **81 images** and on density
of proof, not on illustration.

The drawing vocabulary this page now uses (`draw`, `wipe`, `fill-in`, `grow`, `tick`, `land`) is
**zippyscale.com's**, not Remy's. That is deliberate: this page has 12 images where Remy has 81, so
it has to make its arguments with drawn elements rather than with photography it does not have.

Anyone claiming this page "matches Remy" by adding SVG line drawing is wrong about Remy. What it
takes from him is the restraint: white ground, one accent, monospace display, and proof density.

## Where this page is still behind

1. **Proof density.** 81 images against 12. His Wall of Love is the single strongest thing on either
   page and we have no equivalent asset.
2. **The FAQ and the agenda are still pure paragraphs.** Both fail the internal check that no
   section is only copy.
3. **One ask, one field.** He asks for an email. We ask for three things before anyone has seen
   anything happen.

## Where this page is ahead

1. Every quoted line is sourced to a public post and category matched. Remy's testimonials are
   screenshots with no traceable origin.
2. Every figure carries the account or file it was counted from.
3. Motion is gated on arrival, so nothing finishes above the reader.
