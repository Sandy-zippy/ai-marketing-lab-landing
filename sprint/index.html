The actual problem

You were sold tools. You needed systems.

A tool does one task and then waits for you to prompt it again. So you add another tool, and another, and end up with more tabs, more handoffs and the same amount of work sitting on you.

A system has four parts the tool does not give you. The instructions and standards for the job. The memory, so it does not start from zero. The trigger that starts it without you. And the review point where you approve the output before anyone sees it.

That is the whole gap. It is not a smarter model, and it is not a longer prompt. It is the four parts around the tool, built once, for your business.

Tools do tasks. Systems run the work.

How a week works

Build in the session. Run it in your business. Bring it back.

01

Live build session

The system gets built on screen and you build yours alongside it, on your own offer and your own accounts. Recorded, so a missed week is recoverable.

02

You run it that week

The asset only exists if it runs on real work. You use it, it breaks somewhere, you note where. That is the point of the week.

03

Shipping review

You bring what you built and what broke. It gets reviewed, corrected and closed out before the next asset starts on top of it.

Live. Six group build sessions, one per week, plus the weekly shipping review. Every session is recorded so a missed week is recoverable, but the recordings are the backup and not the product. The product is the six assets running in your accounts.

Wednesdays at 20:00 IST. Session 1 is Wednesday 16 September and session 6 is Wednesday 21 October. Six sessions, one a week, same slot every week.

I am not technical. Will I keep up?

No coding background is required and AI beginners are welcome. Implementation work is required. You will be typing, connecting accounts and fixing your own build. If the phrase "connect your account" makes you want to hire someone else to do it, this format will frustrate you.

The live session, plus the time it takes to run that week's asset on real work in your own accounts. That second part varies a lot depending on how organised your accounts already are. What is fixed is this: if a week goes by with no build, there is no asset at the end of it.
