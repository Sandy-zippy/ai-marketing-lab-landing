Their month

Full calendar this month. Nothing booked for next month?

Think about your last three months. Not the good one. All three.

One month your calendar is full, and it's full because you spent every evening sending DMs, following up and posting. A couple of clients sign. You get busy delivering, which is the part you're actually good at.

About four weeks later you look up and there's nothing in the pipeline. So you go back to chasing.

It isn't that you can't get clients. You can. It's that every client depends on you pushing, and the week you stop pushing, it stops.

That cycle is the whole problem. Here's why the usual fixes didn't break it.

Why it isn't your fault

Why more posts, another agency and AI tools didn't fix it

Getting a client isn't one task. It's a chain. Someone finds the right people and writes to them, then follows up when they don't reply, which is most of the time. Someone makes the ads, builds the page, calls the lead back fast, reminds them before the call and follows up after it.

  • More contentWhen AI arrived, everyone made more posts.More captions and carousels, faster than before. The pipeline looked exactly the same, because more content was never the problem.
  • An agency for the adsOne link fixed. The chain still breaks.The ads bring the lead in. If nobody calls it back fast and follows up, the lead you paid for goes quiet.
  • Doing it all yourselfYou can't run every link while you deliver.In most businesses the someone is you. Nobody can chase and deliver in the same week.
  • A chat windowA prompt writes a caption.It doesn't build the list, write 200 different first messages and follow up on day 3 and day 7.

So the chain breaks, usually at the follow-up, and the lead you already paid to find just goes quiet.

What changed for me

Upstream Downstream: the whole chain, written down once

I stopped trying to run the chain faster and wrote it down instead. Every step I'd done by hand for eight years became a skill file: who to target, what to say, when to follow up, what a good ad looks like for a coach compared with an agency.
