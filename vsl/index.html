01 / The mechanism

Tools do tasks. Systems keep the work moving.

A tool waits for another prompt. A system has the instructions, memory, trigger, review point, and failure path around that tool. The goal is not to replace you with a fictional AI employee. It is to remove the repeated operating work that keeps landing back on you.

01

Outreach

Researches the right prospects and prepares the first useful message for your judgement.

02

Design

Turns a documented brand system into pages and campaign assets without starting from zero.

03

Automation

Moves leads, follow-up, and routine handoffs without a person carrying every step.

04

Paid media

Starts with measurement, then helps you see what to stop, keep, and improve.

05

Content

Turns one useful idea into edited, formatted, and scheduled output for the week.

06

Orchestration

Coordinates the other five from one instruction, with human approval where it matters.

Five systems are settled. Orchestration is the newest and hardest. You can build a working version in week six, but it will keep improving after the cohort. That limitation is part of the offer, not hidden below it.
