## Page copy (as built)

**Callout:** For agency owners, high-ticket coaches and B2B founders who already have paying clients

**H1:** Full calendar when you chase. Empty pipeline when you deliver.

**Under video:** Watch how I broke that cycle with Claude Code. First for clients in Canada and the US, then for my own business in India.

**CTA (one action, repeated 3x):** Book my call, ₹999
**Under CTA:** 45 minutes with me. The ₹999 comes off whichever program you join.

**Proof bar:** Since 2017 / running an agency out of Vancouver. $220,000 / in 6 months in India. ~300 leads, ~50 qualified, 10+ closed / at ₹1.5L each, on my own automation offer.

**The chain (H2: The chain breaks at follow-up):** Getting a client isn't one task. It's a chain, and in most businesses you're every link. So it breaks, usually at the follow-up, and the lead you paid for goes quiet.

**Mechanism (H2: Written once. Run by Claude Code.):** Every step I did by hand for eight years is now a skill file: who to target, what to say, when to follow up. Claude Code runs the file. Your judgement goes into it; you stop being the bottleneck.

**Cases (H2: Clients you're trying to win):** three cards, figures above. Footnote: The last line on each card is what the client paid us.

**Payments (H2: The payments, unedited):** Payment emails from my agency's inbox, as they arrived.

**Two ways (H2: Two ways to build it):** Sprint + Accelerator, no prices. "Which one fits is what the call decides."

**Guarantee (H2: The First Client guarantee):** Follow the build. After launch day, do the outreach every weekday for 30 days. No client closed within 90 days? You get your money back. All of it. Same guarantee on both programs.

**Not for (H2: Who this isn't for):** Freelancers looking for a first project. People who want to become a coach one day. You need a business that exists and an offer people already pay for.

**FAQ:** Why does the call cost ₹999? / What happens on the call? / How much time does it take? / I'm not technical. Will I keep up?

**Close (H2: Picture Friday, 13 November):** It's the last day of the Sprint. Your offer's written, your page is live, and there are calls in your calendar you didn't chase.
