Data deletion

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time. This covers data from our website, our booking and payment pages, and any Facebook or Instagram login or ad form used with the AI Marketing Lab app.

How to ask

Email hello@zippyscale.in with the subject "Data deletion request". Include the name, email and phone number you used with us, so we can find your records.

What happens next

We confirm we received your request within 7 days. We delete your data from our booking, email and analytics records within 30 days and email you when it's done. Payment records we're required to keep by law are kept only for as long as the law requires.
