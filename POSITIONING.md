## 1. Positioning and mechanism

### One line

AI Marketing Labs helps active service businesses build six practical AI marketing systems in six weeks: outreach, design, automation, paid media, content, and orchestration.

### The big idea

Most buyers were sold tools. A tool completes one task and waits for another prompt. Adding more tools often creates more tabs, more handoffs, and more work for the same operator.

A system combines four parts:

1. The tool that performs the task.
2. The instructions and standards for the job.
3. The memory required to avoid starting from zero.
4. The trigger that starts the work without another manual prompt.

The operating sentence is:

> Tools do tasks. Systems run the work.

This is not an AI employee, autonomous marketing team, tool directory, prompt course, or done-for-you service. It is a guided implementation build.

## 2. Promise and limitations

### Promise

Build one working marketing system per week inside the buyer's real accounts and workflows. Each system has a defined input, output, review point, and failure path.

### Limitations that must stay visible

- Buyers continue to approve important work.
- Scope depends on the buyer's accounts, access, data, and existing offer.
- Orchestration is the newest system. The six-week output is a working first version, not full autonomy.
- Tool requirements and expected optional costs must be published before enrollment opens.
- Dates, refund terms, booking, and checkout must be working and visible before money is collected.
- No public guarantee is approved. Do not publish one until written terms are settled.

## 5. Buyer and qualifier

Primary buyer:

- Active service-business owner, coach, agency owner, freelancer, or marketing operator.
- Has a live offer and real lead, client, campaign, or content work.
- Can access the accounts the systems need.
- Will implement and test something every week.

Not a fit:

- Wants another library of AI tips.
- Wants marketing done for them forever.
- Does not have a real offer or workflow yet.
- Cannot make time to test inside the business.

AI beginners are welcome. No coding background is required, but implementation work is required.

## 7. Page jobs

### Homepage

Explain the six-system mechanism, show verified proof with the historical bridge, present the six-week build and two working price points, qualify the buyer, answer objections, and route primarily to the VSL.

### VSL page

When no video is hosted, act as an honest release-list page and state that the recording is being finalized. Once video exists, make playback immediate, keep the page focused, and show the offer only after the mechanism and proof.

### Webinar page

When no session is scheduled, act as a live-session interest list. Once scheduled, show the exact date, time, timezone, duration, three teaching outcomes, reminder consent, and what happens after submission.

### Thank-you page

Confirm only the action that actually occurred. Do not claim a seat, purchase, booking, waitlist position, email delivery, or form storage unless that state is verified.
